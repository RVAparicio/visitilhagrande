## 4. Reddit — 2 drafts de respuesta (firmados, con disclosure)

**r/braziltravel — típico thread "Ilha Grande worth it? how many days?":**
> Local here — I run a small pousada in Vila do Abraão. Honest take: a day trip doesn't make sense (you lose hours to the boats); 2–3 nights minimum, 5–7 ideal. The classic mistakes are underestimating the Lopes Mendes walk (it's 2h30–3h15 one way — walk in, taxi-boat back) and missing the last boat back from remote beaches like Dois Rios (no scheduled boats — leave by 15:30). I keep a free guide with all the boat times/prices at visitilhagrande.com if useful (full disclosure: it's mine, no ads, no bookings).

**r/solotravel o thread de trilhas — "Pico do Papagaio sin guía?":**
> Please don't do the night climb unguided — it's been prohibited for ~two years, there are ~12 unmarked forks, and there's no rescue infrastructure on that mountain (no fire brigade, no rangers who climb). By day it's doable unguided if you leave 7–9 AM. Sunrise = registered (CADASTUR) guide, ~2 AM start, ~R$230–380 pp. I live on the island and wrote up the full logistics here: visitilhagrande.com/ilha-grande-trails/ (disclosure: my guide, free).
